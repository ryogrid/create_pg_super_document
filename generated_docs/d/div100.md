## Overview

## Detailed Description
The `div100` function implements division-by-constant optimization using the multiply-high technique with a more complex approach than `div5` and `div10`. It first performs a right shift by 2 bits on the input, then applies a different magic multiplier (0x28F5C28F5C28F5C3), followed by another right shift by 2 bits.

This two-stage approach is necessary because dividing by 100 (which is 4 × 25) requires a more sophisticated calculation to maintain precision across the full range of 64-bit values. The pre-shift by 2 effectively divides by 4, and the subsequent multiplication and shift complete the division by 25, resulting in the overall division by 100.

This optimization is particularly valuable for decimal formatting operations where rapid division by powers of 10 is frequently required.
